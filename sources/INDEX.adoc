:docidentifier: OIML Bulletin Extracted Publications

This index lists OIML publications whose full text was published in the
`bulletin-data` tree (the OIML Bulletin, 1960–present).

Each entry below links to a Metanorma AsciiDoc rendering of the publication,
transcribed from the corresponding `ocr.md` file in `<year>/<issue>/`.

== Scope

The Bulletin published full Recommendation text only during the 1964–1972
window. After 1972, Recommendations became standalone for-sale publications
and the Bulletin carried status listings only.

All documents below are in French (the OIML working language at the time).
English titles are editorial translations provided for findability.

== Note on the 1970 « revised editions »

The 1970 publications marked "Edition 2" of R 1 and R 2 are genuine revisions
of the 1964 provisional editions (same subject, updated content).

However, R 4, R 5, and R 6 (1970) are *not* revisions of the 1964
provisional Recommendations with the same numbers — OIML reassigned the
numbers at the 3rd CIML Conference (1968):

[cols="1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
|Number |1964 (prov.) subject |1970 (formal) subject

|R 4 |Errors on discontinuous-indicating weighing |Volumetric flasks (one-mark) in glass
|R 5 |Elastic-element pressure gauges (working apparatus) |Volume meters for liquids (other than water)
|R 6 |Blood-pressure instrument manometers |Volume meters for gas — general specifications
|===

== Out of scope

The following classes of OIML publication are *not* in this index:

* **B (Basic Publications), D (Documents), G (Guides), V (Vocabularies)** —
  never published in full text in the Bulletin; standalone from the start.
* **Recommendations first published after 1972** — became standalone
  for-sale publications; the Bulletin carried only status listings.
* **Revised editions issued after 1972** (e.g. R 7 edition 1979) —
  these are sourced from the standalone OIML publications, not the Bulletin.
